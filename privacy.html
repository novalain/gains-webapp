1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Novamitech AB ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Makromat mobile application ("App"). This policy applies to all versions of the app, including free and premium features.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Username, email address, password
  • Profile Information: Age, gender, height, weight, activity level, health goals
  • Nutrition Data: Recipes, meals, calorie intake, macronutrient data
  • User Content: Recipes you create, ingredients you add, comments
  • Subscription Information: Payment history, subscription status, billing address
  • Communication: Feedback, support requests, messages to us

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device Identifiers: Unique device ID, IP address, operating system
  • Usage Statistics: Features used, time in app, navigation patterns
  • Technical Information: App version, device model, screen resolution
  • Error Reports: Crash reports, performance data for app improvement
  • Location Data: Only with your permission, for local features

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • App Store Information: Purchase history, subscription status from Apple/Google
  • Social Media: If you choose to connect your account (name, profile picture)
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized usage statistics from analytics services

2.4 Third-Party Software/Service Providers

We may use UXCam, which is an analytics solution. UXCam may record:

  • Screens visited
  • Interaction patterns (such as screen actions, gestures: taps, scrolls)
  • Device details (Type, Version, Model, Operating System)

We are using the information collected by UXCam to improve our app.

UXCam does not collect personally identifiable information and does not track your browsing habits across apps. For more information see Privacy Policy for Information Collected by the UXCam Service.

3.1 Core Service Functions

  • Provide and maintain the Makromat app
  • Create and manage your user account
  • Calculate nutritional content and calorie intake
  • Save your recipes and dietary preferences
  • Sync data across your devices

3.2 Subscriptions and Payments

  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Provide access to premium features
  • Send billing confirmations
  • Manage subscription renewals and cancellations
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized use

3.3 Improvement and Personalization

  • Personalize your app experience
  • Provide recommendations based on your preferences
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the app
  • Develop new features and services

3.4 Communication

  • Provide customer service and support
  • Send important notifications about your subscription
  • Inform about app updates and new features
  • Send security notifications and service updates

4. Information Sharing

4.1 We DO NOT share your personal information with third parties for marketing

4.2 We may share information in the following limited cases:

  • With your explicit consent: When you explicitly approve sharing
  • Service Providers: With trusted partners who help us operate the app (hosting, payments, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transactions: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of our assets
  • Safety Protection: To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Anonymized Data: Aggregated, de-identified statistics for research and development

5. Community Features and Public Information

When you use our community features:

  • Public Recipes: Recipes you choose to share become available to other users
  • Username: Your username may be displayed in connection with your contributions
  • Comments and Ratings: Can be seen by other users
  • Control: You can change visibility or remove your contributions at any time
  • Moderation: We reserve the right to remove inappropriate content

6. Data Security and Protection

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

6.1 Technical Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Intrusion detection and firewall protection
  • Secure data storage on certified servers

6.2 Organizational Measures

  • Limited access to personal information on a "need-to-know" basis
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Staff training on data security and privacy
  • Incident response procedures for security breaches

7. Data Storage and Retention

7.1 Storage Periods

  • Active Accounts: Data is stored as long as your account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Data may be retained for up to 3 years after last login
  • Subscription Data: Payment history retained according to accounting legislation (7 years)
  • Support Communication: Retained for 2 years for quality assurance
  • Anonymized Data: May be retained indefinitely for research

7.2 Data Deletion

You can request to have your data deleted at any time. We will:

  • Delete your personal information within 30 days
  • Retain only data required by law
  • Anonymize data used for statistics
  • Notify you when deletion is complete

7.3 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time by using the in-app delete function or by contacting us at michael@makromat.app. When we receive your deletion request, we remove personal data associated with your account unless retention is required for legal purposes.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

8.1 Basic Rights

  • Right to Information: To receive information about how we process your data
  • Right of Access: To obtain a copy of all your personal information
  • Right to Rectification: To correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure: To have your information deleted ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restriction: To limit the processing of your information

8.2 Advanced Rights

  • Right to Data Portability: To receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: To object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Rights Regarding Automated Decision-Making: Protection against decisions based solely on automated processing

8.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at michael@makromat.app with "GDPR Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days.
